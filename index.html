    Event log

        The archive keeps every event that reached an orange or red alert, M6+ earthquakes, named storms, events covered by Moody's RMS or Verisk, and any event with a published loss figure. Loss figures are quoted from the named estimator and updated hourly as new estimates appear.

        Positions and alert levels come straight from each agency's feed. GDACS and USGS PAGER alerts estimate humanitarian impact (green → red). Volcano entries come from the most recent weekly report, so they lag other feeds. Follow official local warnings for any decision about safety.
